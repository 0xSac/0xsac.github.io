0x0200 // Operational flow

The 1-Day Pilot format

  1. PHASE I

    Audit & Harden

    Teams baseline telemetry, inventory their pod, and remediate vulnerabilities before the adversary cell goes live.

    →

  2. PHASE II

    Live-Fire Defense

    Defenders contain an active intrusion while executives work the synchronized crisis simulation fed by range telemetry.

    →

  3. PHASE III

    Joint Hotwash / AAR

    Red, Blue, and Executive participants reconcile what happened, what was decided, and what to fix. Scores and CPEs are issued.
